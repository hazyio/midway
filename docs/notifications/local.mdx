---
title: "Local notifications"
description: "Show Midway notifications on your computer"
---

Midway can show notifications through your operating system's desktop notification service. No webhook or token is required. Local notifications are not enabled by default.

## Enable local notifications

Add `Local` to `notification.send_from` in `midway.yaml`:

```yaml
notification:
	send_from:
		- Local
	pick_from: All
```

If you already have other notification methods in `send_from`, keep them and add `Local` to the list.

Local notifications require a desktop session with system notifications enabled. They may not appear when Midway runs in a headless environment.
